## LAB 11 – Canva Magic Studio Aviation Mini-Campaign

This lab focused on creating an **AI-assisted aviation mini-campaign using Canva Magic Studio**. The main objective was to understand how AI-generated visuals and Canva design tools can be combined to create consistent, professional and responsible communication materials for an aviation audience.

The campaign was based on a fictional organisation, **AeroVista Airports**, with the campaign name **“Travel Smart at the Airport.”** Its objective was to encourage domestic passengers to arrive prepared and follow verified airport instructions. The campaign used the same brand identity across three formats: a square social-media post, a portrait passenger-awareness poster and a 16:9 landscape presentation visual.

A simple **brand guide** was developed using dark blue, sky blue and warm yellow, along with Montserrat Bold for headings and Open Sans for body text. The visual style was modern, clean and professional, while the communication tone was helpful, calm and trustworthy.

Canva’s **Magic Media** was used to generate an airport visual showing passengers preparing for travel and checking official flight information. The generated image was reviewed for unrealistic elements, unclear text, personal information, logos and aviation inaccuracies. **Magic Expand** was then used to adapt the visual from a square format to a landscape presentation format, followed by a review for duplicated people, distorted objects and inconsistent backgrounds. **Magic Edit** was used to improve an unclear digital display and make it neutral.

The campaign was adapted into a social-media post, passenger-awareness poster and presentation visual while maintaining consistent colours, fonts, campaign name and core message. A brand-and-accuracy audit was also completed to check content accuracy, privacy, professionalism and responsible AI use.

The lab also included an original campaign, **“Check In Smart,”** focused on encouraging passengers to use official digital check-in channels and verify travel information before reaching the airport.

Overall, the activity demonstrated the importance of **AI-assisted design, brand consistency, accuracy checking, responsible communication, peer review and human verification** when creating aviation-related marketing materials.
